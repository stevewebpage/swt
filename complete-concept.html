Before You Shoot

Pre-Shoot Checklist

Get the Challenger detailed and cleaned. Every inch gleaming. Racing stripes sharp. This car is a co-star.
Print the giant Walmart gift card. Google "Walmart gift card image" · Print 11x17 or larger · FedEx Office / Staples / Walmart Photo Center · ~$5–10 on cardstock or foam board.
Buy the real Walmart gift card ($200) and gas card. Have the real cards on you during filming. Giant print card for the reveal — real cards for credibility.
Suit pressed and ready. Sharp. Clean. Unexpected. The contrast between the suit and the gas pump is everything.
Friend confirmed to film. Handheld is fine. Slightly loose and natural is better than stiff and produced. They follow you from car to pump.
Giveaway page live on GitHub Pages. Test stevewebpage.github.io/swt/giveaway.html before shooting. Also test musicrow.us/giveaway to see if path forwarding works. Make sure form works on mobile.
TikTok bio link updated. Use musicrow.us/giveaway if path forwarding works — otherwise use stevewebpage.github.io/swt/giveaway.html. Test before posting the video.
Read the script out loud three times. Then forget it. Speak naturally. Your natural delivery IS the hook.

Delivery

How To Perform It

  • Energy high from the very first word. No warmup. No intro. WIN THIS PRIZE is the first sound they hear.
  • The card reveal is the money moment. Keep it hidden below frame. Lift it dramatically. Make it feel like a game show.
  • One take if possible. Raw and real wins on TikTok every time. Imperfect IS perfect.
  • Neighbor over the fence energy. Not a commercial. Not a pitch. A real person talking to a real person.
  • "You have my word on that" — say this slowly. Mean it. That line builds more trust than any legal disclaimer ever could.
  • Total run time: 25–30 seconds. No longer. TikTok rewards completion rate.
  • The Challenger does half the work without a word. Let it sit in the background. Don't explain it. Let them wonder.
90-Day Plan

The Monthly Posting Cycle

  • Day 1 — Launch. The main video. Suit. Challenger. Gas pump. WIN THIS PRIZE.
  • Day 7 — Show the prize. Close up of the real cards. "This is real. This is waiting for somebody."
  • Day 14 — Tell your story. Who is Steve Bickham? War Pigs. Crazy Boy. The tears on the recording. 30 seconds.
  • Day 21 — Reminder. "Halfway through the month. Still free. Still waiting for a winner."
  • Day 28 — Last chance. Final push. Urgency. "Few days left."
  • Day 30 — Winner announcement. Real person. Real prize. Public proof it happened. Drives next month's entries.

The winner announcement is as powerful as the launch video. Real proof drives real trust. Real trust drives the next wave of entries. Run this for 90 days and watch what happens.

Legal — Read Before Launch

Keep It Clean & Legal

  • "No purchase necessary" must appear on every video and on the entry form. Already done.
  • Award the prize every single month. On time. Publicly. No exceptions. This is both legal and essential for trust.
  • "Not affiliated with TikTok or Walmart" in the on-screen text.
  • One entry per person. State this clearly on the form.
  • Keep winner records. Name, email, date awarded. Simple spreadsheet is fine.
  • Under $5,000 prize value means no state registration required anywhere in the US.
  • The entry form privacy promise is binding. Never sell or share the list. You gave your word on camera.
