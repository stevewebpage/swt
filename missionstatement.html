A Statement from the Artist
🌲

In a world flooded with auto-tuned voices and AI-generated tracks, some of us still believe music should make you feel something real.

I'm Steve Bickham — a roots musician from Lebanon, Tennessee, writing and recording songs the old way. No algorithms wrote these melodies. No computer generated these emotions. Just a guitar, a story, and forty-plus years of living behind every note.

MusicRow.US was built as a front porch — a place where real music lives, where families can gather, where faith and community still mean something. A clean space in a noisy world.

This music isn't chasing charts. It's chasing you — the person who still believes a song should pull at your heart, not just fill the background.

Come sit a spell. The forest is always open.

— Steven Douglas Bickham 🌲 MusicRow.US
